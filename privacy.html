How the quiz handles your data

Clinician AI Habits Check · The Efficient GP · Dr David Stokes · Last updated October 2026

By default, nothing leaves your browser

Your answers and results are worked out on your own device. If you close the page without ticking the consent box, nothing is sent anywhere.

If you choose to add your answers

Only this is sent and stored:

  • your 18 answers, as numbers from 1 to 4
  • your six habit levels
  • your role and main AI tool, only if you choose them
  • the month you took part (not the day or time)
  • the version of the quiz

We never collect your name, email address, practice, location or any free text, and the page sets no cookies.

Why

To describe, in aggregate, how clinicians use AI, for The Efficient GP newsletter, podcast and talks. Results are only ever reported for groups of 10 or more people, and never by practice or organisation. The data is not used for marketing and is never sold or shared with employers.

Where it is stored

In a database run by Supabase, hosted in London, UK. Like most web services, Supabase keeps short-lived technical logs (which include IP addresses) to run and protect the service. These logs are not linked to your answers and are not used by us.

Withdrawing

Because nothing links your answers to you, we can't find or delete a single response afterwards. Please only tick the box if you're happy with that.
